Using the observatory

Use the Plaza sidebar to focus its network, or All Plazas to compare environments. Agent identities are scoped to each Plaza.

Click a graph node or table row to inspect it. Drag nodes to arrange the graph, drag the background to pan, and scroll to zoom. Dragged nodes stay pinned until Relayout. Fit graph changes the view without moving nodes. Fit entire graph clears filters and selection to show all authorized agents and Plazas. Select a Plaza node to inspect its monitoring connection, registered members, health, jobs and runtime metrics. Registration lines show membership; arrows show observed calls. Details require monitoring permission for that Plaza's runtime. Interaction links use up to seven days of history (10,000 completed calls per agent). New trace events do not remove links. Hover an arrow to see when it was last observed.

Use + in Plaza Network to add a Plaza URL. Connect opens its monitoring approval flow. Saved Plazas follow your account across browsers; Remove deletes an entry you added. HTTPS is required outside this computer.

Filter by node name, PIT types, Plaza, status or call count. Min and Max are inclusive; blank means no limit. Counts total incoming and outgoing observed calls across the authorized topology before display filters; self calls count once. Registration lines do not count as calls. The Calls column and node tooltip show these totals. Choose several types in the dropdown, or toggle their legend buttons. All PIT types clears the type filter. Selection emphasizes immediate incoming and outgoing neighbors within the current filters. Neighbors only hides unrelated agents; Focus selection zooms to that neighborhood. The icons at the top right inside the graph fit the current view, fit the entire graph and clear filters, reset the layout, and maximize or restore the topology. Hover for labels. Escape restores it. Move Density left for more space or right for a tighter layout. It keeps your zoom, selection and dragged nodes; use Fit graph to bring the full layout into view. Relayout releases dragged positions. Density follows your account across browsers. Play at the lower right enables a brief pulse from caller to receiver when new calls arrive in a dashboard refresh (every 10 seconds). Pause stops the pulses. Existing calls are not replayed. Reduced motion highlights the affected edge without moving a pulse.

Select a communication arrow (or focus it and press Enter or Space) to inspect its directional call counts, failures, timing and related jobs. The endpoints are emphasized; Focus selection fits both. Jobs shows active and finished jobs matched by job ID, trace or peer endpoint, with trace and authorized log actions. View logs opens a popup for the selected job and keeps the Jobs list in place. Agent jobs are shown by default; use Activity to inspect HTTP requests. Filter by state, outcome, name or ID, and the past number of minutes for finished work. Load older finished jobs for more history. Job filters follow your account across browsers and are independent of the graph's call window. Completion records are retained for up to seven days, with 1,000 jobs and 1,000 requests per reporting agent. Only reported completion evidence is shown; older missing history cannot be reconstructed. Registration lines connect members to their Plaza; they do not represent communication.

Health is reported by the agent; connectivity comes from Plaza receipt times. A faded node has stale or missing telemetry. Legacy agents show “Unknown” until upgraded.

Interactions show observed outbound calls in the retained event window. Set Past to a whole number of minutes or hours (up to 7 days) to count only calls completed during that rolling period. Leave it blank to use retained history. Each Plaza calculates its counts before graph filters; Refresh and automatic updates move the time window forward. Traces may be incomplete when an agent is offline. Jobs and logs may contain private details and require ownership or Plaza administrator access.

Allow one heartbeat cycle for logs to arrive. Previously retrieved logs remain available as labeled cached results when an agent is offline. New requests wait until the agent responds or the request expires. Live tail polls while its panel is open.
