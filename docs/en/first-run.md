# First run

1. **Choose your language.**
2. **Sign in to tw-001.** The sign-in screen says **Sign in to use tw-001-olive-canyon.retis.ai**. Signing in and creating an account both happen inside Retis Terminal; no web page opens.
   - New here: choose **Create an account**, enter your email and a password (the name is optional), then **Create account**. Open the link in the confirmation email (check spam), then sign in back in Retis Terminal.
   - Have an account: enter your account or email and password, then **Sign in**.
   - Your computer gets access automatically, with no wait for approval.
3. **Taiwan Semiconductor Watch and Strategy Monitor open by themselves** after your first sign-in, in a workspace called **Getting started** (it can take about a minute). The Watch is in front: quotes, charts, news and company data for five Taiwan chip stocks, with no points needed for quotes and news. Asking an analyst to comment on a news item uses points, only when you ask. Strategy Monitor is open behind it.
4. **Install Local yfinance.** Charts, news and price history come from [Local yfinance](local-yfinance.md) on your computer: choose **New Data Browser**, open **Retis MAS Manager** under **On this computer**, and choose **Install** on the Local yfinance row. You do this once. Until then, the Watch shows quotes but no chart or news. If you can't install it, add your own key for a price-history source such as Fugle instead.
5. **Try the example.** Click Strategy Monitor, choose **Try the example: 2330.TW / 2303.TW pairs trading**, then **Refresh** to see both stocks' latest prices. The example reads prices from Local yfinance, or from a source you added your own key for. With neither, it says how to get one. Creating the example and updating prices are free.

You start with **1,000 points**, topped up to 1,000 on the 1st of each month (Taiwan time). See [Points](points.md) and [Strategy Monitor basics](strategy-monitor.md).

## Opening it again

Double-click Retis Terminal (or choose **Open** from the menu-bar or system-tray icon). It returns to your last workspace without signing in again.
