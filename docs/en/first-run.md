# First run

1. **Choose your language.**
2. **Sign in to tw-001.** Choose **Sign in to tw-001-olive-canyon.retis.ai** (the button shows tw-001's address); the tw-001 site opens in a new browser tab. Create an account (and click the link in the verification email; check spam) or sign in. Back in Retis Terminal you're signed in within seconds: your computer gets access automatically, with no wait for approval.
3. **Taiwan Semiconductor Watch and Strategy Monitor are already open** on your first sign-in. The Watch is in front: quotes, charts, news and company data for five Taiwan chip stocks, with no points needed for quotes and news. Asking an analyst to comment on a news item uses points, only when you ask. Strategy Monitor is open behind it.
4. **Try the example.** Click Strategy Monitor, choose **Try the example: 2330.TW / 2303.TW pairs**, then **Refresh** to see both stocks' latest prices. Creating the example uses 1 point, and so does each price update.

You start with **1,000 points**, topped up to 1,000 on the 1st of each month (Taiwan time). See [Points](points.md) and [Strategy Monitor basics](strategy-monitor.md).
