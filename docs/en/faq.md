# FAQ

- **Mac says it can't verify the developer:** System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway** (first time only).
- **Windows protected your PC:** **More info** → **Run anyway** (first time only).
- **No verification email:** check spam, then use **Resend verification email** on tw-001's sign-in page.
- **Closed the browser tab:** menu-bar or system-tray icon → **Open**.
- **Where is my data?** Workspaces, layouts and kept results stay on your computer; tw-001 keeps your account, points and access records. See [Privacy](../../legal/PRIVACY.en.md).
- **Real orders?** No. The beta paper trades only.
- **Data provider keys (Fugle, FinMind):** optional, in **Terminal settings → Data sources**. Keys stay on your computer and go only to that provider.
