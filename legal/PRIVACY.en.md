# Privacy

Notice under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (Art. 8) and Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

**Who:** Retis AI Pte Ltd ("Retis"). Personal-data requests: [privacy@retis.ai](mailto:privacy@retis.ai). Questions go to [Discussions](https://github.com/alvincho/retis-terminal-beta/discussions) and problems to [Issues](https://github.com/alvincho/retis-terminal-beta/issues/new/choose) on GitHub.

**Kept on tw-001 by Retis:**
- **Account:** email, username, display name, email verification status, sign-up and last sign-in times. Passwords are stored hashed by the sign-in service; Retis never sees them.
- **Points:** your balance and each change.
- **Access:** your Retis Terminal's identifier, name and when it got access; agents you register.
- **Logs:** site access logs (without query strings or credentials), for operations and abuse prevention.
- **AI analysis:** when you use it, your request is sent to the AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result.

**Kept only on your computer:** workspaces, layouts, kept results, preferences, and any data-provider API keys you enter (encrypted; sent only to that provider, never to Retis or any agent). Removing Retis Terminal and its folder deletes them.

**Purpose, period, region, recipients:** to run and maintain the Service, count points, prevent abuse and send verification and service email; kept while your account exists and deleted within 30 days after you close it unless the law requires otherwise; hosted in the AWS Asia Pacific (Taipei) region (ap-east-2); shared only with service providers Amazon Web Services (hosting, email) and Anthropic (only when you use AI analysis). Retis does not sell personal data.

**Your rights:** ask to see, get a copy of, correct, stop processing or delete your data by emailing [privacy@retis.ai](mailto:privacy@retis.ai). Never post personal data in a public issue. Without an email address you can't create an account.
